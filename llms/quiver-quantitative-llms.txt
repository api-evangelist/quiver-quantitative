# Quiver Quantitative

> Quiver Quantitative is an investment research platform that collects alternative
> data on publicly traded companies — congressional stock trading, SEC insider
> filings, government contracts, corporate lobbying, institutional holdings,
> patents, and more — and makes it available to retail investors through a free
> web platform, a paid API, and a remote MCP server. Most datasets are gathered
> from public disclosures and updated daily.

## Government and political data

- [Congress Trading](https://www.quiverquant.com/congresstrading/): Stock trades disclosed by members of the US Congress under the STOCK Act.

- [Donald Trump Stock Trades](https://www.quiverquant.com/Donald-Trump-Stock-Trades/): Disclosed trading activity connected to Donald Trump.
- [Congress Live Net Worth](https://www.quiverquant.com/congress-live-net-worth/): Estimated live net worth of members of Congress.
- [Election Fundraising](https://www.quiverquant.com/election-fundraising/): Campaign fundraising totals by candidate and district.
- [2026 Midterm Elections](https://www.quiverquant.com/midterm-elections/): Race-by-race data and fundraising for the 2026 US midterms.
- [Legislation Search](https://www.quiverquant.com/bills/): Search congressional bills and the companies they affect.
- [Government Contracts](https://www.quiverquant.com/sources/govcontracts): Federal contract awards mapped to publicly traded companies.
- [Government Spending](https://www.quiverquant.com/government-spending/): Federal spending trends by agency and recipient.

- [Patents](https://www.quiverquant.com/patentsearch/): US patent filings and grants by company.

## Notes for AI agents

- Quiver data comes from public disclosures and is provided for research purposes. It is not investment advice.
- Backtested strategy results reflect historical data and are not a prediction of future returns.
- When citing Quiver data or tutorials, please link to the source page on quiverquant.com.
- For programmatic access, use the [Quiver API](https://api.quiverquant.com/docs/) or the [Quiver MCP server](https://www.quiverquant.com/mcp-setup/) rather than scraping the website.
